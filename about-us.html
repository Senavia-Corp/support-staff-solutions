Alejandro Amado

General Manager

Alejandro Amado created Support Staff Solutions USA LLC in May 2019. He has been contributing with his more than 25 years of leadership experience, a trajectory of competitive growth among US employment agencies, and hiring temporary staff on assignment for recognized logistics companies in Florida with his employment agency. Alejandro Amado has extensive experience in flexible staffing solutions, commitment to quality for companies, and focuses on delivering employees on mission to logistics companies in record time and cost-effective recruiting services. All employees are covered with a workers’ compensation assurance.

As General Manager and CIO for the Americas, he has the important role of directing the company with financial and corporate strategies, putting employees on mission in logistics business operations, and directing the entire company including his first company created in 2007 in Colombia called Gestion Temporal de Colombia S.A.S. Alejandro Amado leads a team of more than 250 people, including professionals and employees on mission in important logistics companies in the United States and Latin America (Colombia).

Helping people from many countries achieve their financial aspirations and family goals, bringing daily money to their homes.

Alejandro works with his entire financial, IT, executive and field team to develop long and short-term growth strategies and company financial objectives. He is committed to expanding Support Staff Solutions USA LLC worldwide, carrying its mission and social commitment to provide employment to all people who have tenacity, attitude, enthusiasm, motivation, and the desire to get ahead.

Alejandro Amado is a lawyer from the Libre University of Bogota, Colombia (1999). He is a specialist in human resources, commercial law, and corporate law. He worked at the Superintendency of Companies for 7 years and at the Amados law firm, in Colombia.
